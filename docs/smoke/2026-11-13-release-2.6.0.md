## Change

Doubao Work can now claim completed campaign rewards:

- `POST /doubao/tasks/claim` accepts `uid` and `campaign_code`.
- Cached tasks retain `task_id`, `instance_task_id`, `recommend_prompts`, and
  `campaign_task_data`.
- The dashboard shows a Claim button for `task_status=2`.
- Upstream `biz_error` responses are passed through instead of being reported
  as success.
- A successful claim updates the local task state and refreshes the Doubao
  overview.
